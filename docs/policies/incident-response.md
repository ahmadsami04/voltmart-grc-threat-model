# VoltMart Electronics: Proposed Incident Response Policy

## Purpose

Establish a proposed process for identifying, reporting, responding to, and learning from information security incidents.

## Scope

This policy would apply to suspected incidents involving customer information, web applications, inventory services, POS systems, employee accounts, and supporting infrastructure.

## Proposed Incident Response Process

### 1. Preparation

Define incident response responsibilities, escalation contacts, communication procedures, and evidence-handling requirements.

### 2. Identification

Review monitoring alerts, suspicious authentication activity, unauthorized changes, and reports from employees or service providers.

### 3. Assessment

Determine the affected systems, potential information exposure, operational impact, and incident severity.

### 4. Containment

Take proportionate steps to limit further damage while preserving relevant evidence and business continuity.

### 5. Eradication and Recovery

Address identified causes, restore affected services safely, and validate that systems are operating as expected.

### 6. Lessons Learned

Document the incident timeline, response decisions, corrective actions, and opportunities for improvement.

## Reporting and Escalation

Employees should report suspected incidents through an approved organizational reporting channel.

Significant incidents should be escalated to designated security and management personnel. Applicable legal, contractual, and regulatory notification requirements should be evaluated.

## Related Risks

- TH-10: Customer information disclosure.
- TH-13: Online storefront denial of service.
- TH-16: Compromised workstation privilege escalation.
- TH-17: Web application privilege escalation.

## Related ISO 27001 Annex A Controls

- A.5.24 Information security incident management planning and preparation
- A.8.15 Logging
- A.8.16 Monitoring activities

## Proposed Evidence

- Incident reports and response records.
- Security event logs.
- Investigation timelines.
- Corrective action records.
- Recovery validation results.

## Review and Ownership

The designated incident response owner should review this policy periodically and after significant incidents or exercises.

## Status and Limitations

This policy is a fictional proposal. No actual incident response process has been deployed or tested as part of this project.
